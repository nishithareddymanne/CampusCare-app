Built for the way a campus actually works

Simple for students, structured for administrators.

Quick complaint form

Title, category, location and an optional photo. Takes about a minute.

Live status tracking

See whether a complaint is pending, assigned, in progress or resolved.

Department routing

Administrators assign each complaint to Maintenance, Hostel, Laboratory or Facilities.

Full history

Every status change is recorded with who made it, when, and why.

Admin dashboard

Charts by category, department and status show where problems pile up.

Private by design

Students only see their own complaints. Administrator tools are locked to administrators.

How it works

From report to resolution in four steps.

Register

Create a student account with your college email.

Raise a complaint

Describe the issue, pick a category and add a photo if it helps.

Admin assigns it

An administrator routes it to the responsible department.

Follow it to resolution

Read each remark as the work progresses until it is resolved.

Know where your complaint stands

Every complaint moves through the same clear stages. A resolved complaint stays resolved, and a rejected one always comes with a reason.

Pending› Assigned› In Progress› Resolved or Rejected

Open My Complaints after logging in to search, filter and view the complete timeline of any complaint.
